Seven days. Any stack. One browser tab is all you need to ship something strange and a little bit great.

Smoking Fish is a fully online web dev hackathon for people who like their ideas a bit smoky. Join solo or with a team of up to four, build a working website or web app from scratch, and share it with the community. Beginners and veterans are both welcome.

The schedule

7 – 14 November. Theme drops on day one.

7 Nov

Kickoff

Theme is announced live. Find a team in the chat and start sketching.

7–14 Nov

Build

Write code, break things, fix them. Mentors answer questions in the help channels all week.

14 Nov

Submit

Push your repo, share a live link and a short walkthrough video. Judges and the community vote on the winners online.

Ground rules

Any stack

React, Svelte, plain HTML or something you made up last night. Use what you like.

Fresh code

Start your project after kickoff. Libraries and open-source tools are fair game.

Teams of 1–4

Go solo or squad up. Everyone on the team must register.

Be kind

Our code of conduct applies in every channel. Break the code, not each other.

Some ideas smell better than others.
